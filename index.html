You started as a guttering company, added roofing in 2020, and dropped "Guttering" from the name entirely in April 2025. Your SIC code is now 43910, roofing activities. The legal entity has already made the move you are describing.

The gap

Your website and your directory listings have not followed. Roof cleaning and gutter cleaning still sit at equal weight with roofing on the homepage. Your Checkatrade profile surfaces under Cleaning Services rather than Roofing — the public page title reads "Willingtons Roofing & Building Ltd | Cleaning Services | Leatherhead, Guildford & Woking".

Checkatrade is where Surrey homeowners shortlist by trade. Someone in Cobham planning a re-roof filters for a roofer. If your primary category is cleaning, you are not in that list. Log in and check your categories — it is free and it takes ten minutes. Worth confirming inside your own account before acting on it.

This is not a criticism of your positioning. It is the observation that you already decided to reposition sixteen months ago and the marketing never caught up.

The same pattern runs through your own site. Your six services are Roofing, Chimneys, Roof Cleaning, Fascia Soffit & Cladding, Flat Roofs, and Guttering & Gutter Cleaning. Roof cleaning and gutter cleaning sit at equal weight with roofing. Your enquiry form has a category dropdown, which means visitors are being invited to self-select into the cheap work.

You told us you want full roofs at around ten thousand pounds, and commercial. Neither has a page. There is no "full roof replacement" service, no "new roof" page, and no commercial page, even though you do commercial work.

Why this matters more for you than for most

You doubled turnover last year, so the top of your funnel works. When a business with a working funnel wants bigger jobs, adding more leads makes the problem worse, not better — it adds more of what you already have too much of. The lever is not volume. It is what the site invites people to ask for.

That is also why fifteen marketing firms have not fixed it. Most of them sell volume.

What fifteen firms have not told you

The honest version

You probably do not need more leads. A business that doubled turnover in a year has a working front end. If the sixteenth agency sells you volume, you will get more gutter cleaning enquiries and be busier for less money.

The three things that would actually change your average job value cost almost nothing: fix the Checkatrade category, put your reviews on your own site, and turn two of your existing before-and-after sets into full roof case studies with the price band on them. None of that is a retainer. It is a Tuesday.

Advertising is worth having after that, because then the money is buying enquiries for ten thousand pound roofs rather than three hundred pound gutters. Doing it in the other order is how the last fifteen went.

Checkatrade reviewers describe you as premium priced. That is not a weakness for the work you want — it is the positioning. But a premium price needs premium proof on the page where the decision happens, and right now your website makes no argument for it at all.

If you are at the top of that band, moving average job value up by even fifteen percent is worth more than any increase in lead volume, and it costs nothing to do. Priority order

Ranked by effect on average job value.

  1. Fix the Checkatrade category

    Free, ten minutes, and it decides whether you appear when a Surrey homeowner filters for a roofer. Nothing else on this list beats it for effort against return.

  2. Build the full roof replacement page

    The job you want needs a page of its own, with the process, the timescale, the guarantee and an honest price band. At present a homeowner planning a re-roof has nothing to read.

  3. Turn your before-and-afters into case studies

    You already have the photographs. Add the property type, the scope, how long it took and roughly what it cost. Two of these will do more for job size than any advertising campaign.

  4. Put your proof on your own website

    Rating, review count, FMB, Checkatrade, Which? Trusted Trader, insurance and guarantee, above the point where someone decides to make contact.

  5. Give commercial its own page and route

    Different buyer, different language, different enquiry form. Currently invisible.

  6. Confirm conversion tracking before spending on ads

    GTM is there. Make sure form submissions and calls are firing as conversions, so the sixteenth agency can be measured in a way the first fifteen were not.
